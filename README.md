# Attention-Steered Earbuds

DIMT 3YP, Oxford Engineering Science, 2026–27.

Arshdeep Kalsi · Billy Adamson · Cody · Maxime Detouy

A music player for people with hearing loss that uses EEG to work out which part of a song the listener is following, and brings that part forward in the mix.

## Research areas (from MT3)

- Synthetic EEG data: can simulated EEG train an attention decoder? (Billy)
- Attention decoding: how accurately and quickly can musical attention be decoded? (Cody)
- Sound separation: how can sources be separated and enhanced? (Arshdeep)
- EEG with other tech: can EEG combine with other sensors for better results? (Maxime)
